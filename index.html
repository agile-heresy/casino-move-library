What is Casino dancing?

Casino is a social partner dance style (sometimes danced in a group called a rueda) that originated in Havana, Cuba in the 1950s, danced to timba music. In a rueda, couples dance in a circle and switch partners as a cantante (caller) shouts out named moves. Learn more at Son y Casino.
